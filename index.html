Creative Arts Department

Welcome to the Creative Arts program at Siena College. We offer a dynamic and flexible curriculum designed to support both traditional and modern artistic practices.

Program Start Dates

  • Fall Semester: 5 September 2024
  • Winter Semester: 7 January 2025
  • Spring Semester: 11 April 2025

Students are encouraged to begin their studies at the start of an academic semester for the best experience.

Available Courses

  • Modern Digital Art and Its Cultural Impact
  • Interactive Media Art and Virtual Reality
  • Sculpture Techniques: From Clay to Digital Models

One highlighted course is "Chemical Reactions in Art", exploring materials such as Glucose (C6H12O6) and their role in art conservation techniques.

Artistic Inspirations

Impressionism

Known for thin brush strokes and an emphasis on light, this movement reshaped outdoor painting.

Abstract Expressionism

Encouraged freedom of expression through non-representational forms.

Pop Art

Bridged fine art and popular culture using mass-media imagery.
